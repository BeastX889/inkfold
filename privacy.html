Inkfold Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 · LumioLab

Short version: your files never leave your device. Inkfold has no accounts, no servers that see your documents, no ads and no tracking.

Your files

Every PDF and image you open in Inkfold is processed entirely on your own computer. Signing, merging, splitting, compressing, protecting, watermarking, numbering, converting and cropping all run locally. Files are not uploaded anywhere, and Inkfold cannot see them.

What is stored on your device

  • Signatures and initials you choose to remember, plus a few preferences (like theme and last-used options). These stay in the app’s local storage on this device, and you can delete them at any time inside the app.
  • The app’s own files, cached so it works offline.

Information we collect

None. Inkfold does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, usage data or analytics. It works with no internet connection at all after it is installed.

Children

Inkfold does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
